Our Commitment

Reliable Support Throughout Every Stage

From initial quotation to production, delivery and after-sales support, our team is committed to providing clear communication, practical technical assistance and dependable service.

01

Warranty Support

Under proper storage, installation, maintenance and operating conditions, products that cannot function normally because of manufacturing-quality issues are covered by our warranty support.

Depending on the condition of the product and the nature of the issue, support may include repair, replacement parts, product replacement or another appropriate solution.

02

Initial Response Within 24 Hours

After receiving a customer inquiry, quality complaint or technical-support request, our team will provide an initial response within 24 hours.

Urgent issues will be reviewed as quickly as possible based on the available product information and operating conditions.

03

Technical Documentation

Relevant technical documents and product records can be provided according to customer, order and project requirements.

  • Product specifications and dimensional information
  • Material information and available material certificates
  • Product certificates and qualification documents
  • Inspection records or reports, when available
  • Packaging and shipment documentation
04

Technical Assistance

According to the product, application and contractual requirements, we can provide technical assistance before and after delivery.

  • Product selection consultation
  • Installation guidance
  • Commissioning assistance
  • Maintenance consultation
  • Replacement-part recommendations
  • Technical training when required
05

Engineering Project Support

For larger engineering and industrial projects, we can review tender documents, drawings, specifications and inspection requirements before production.

Production planning, testing, inspection and acceptance procedures can be coordinated according to confirmed technical requirements and contractual agreements.

06

Quality-Control Procedures

Raw-material purchasing, component manufacturing, dimensional inspection, final verification, packaging and documentation are managed according to our established quality-control procedures.

Additional inspection requirements can be discussed during the quotation and technical-review stage.

After-Sales Service

How We Handle Service Requests

When a product issue occurs, customers may contact our sales or service team by phone or email. Providing complete information helps us evaluate the issue and respond more efficiently.

Please prepare the following information:

  • Product model or item name
  • Product specification and material
  • Application and operating conditions
  • Description of the issue
  • Purchase date or order reference
  • Photos or videos, when available
01

Submit Information

Send the relevant product details, operating information and supporting images.

02

Technical Review

Our team reviews the reported condition and identifies possible causes.

03

Solution Proposal

We provide technical advice, repair guidance, replacement parts or another suitable solution.

04

Follow-Up

We follow up with the customer to confirm the result and close the service request.
